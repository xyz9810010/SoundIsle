# SoundIsle Review Evidence Policy

## Purpose
Future review cycles must improve the project, not merely increase review count.

## Required Attack Format
Every proposed change must state:
1. Existing rule being challenged
2. Concrete failure scenario
3. Why current design cannot handle it
4. Proposed correction
5. New risks introduced
6. Verification method

## Outcomes
- CHANGE: proven defect, correction accepted
- COMPROMISE: proven conflict, balanced correction
- NO_CHANGE: attack did not prove defect
- REJECT: proposal makes system worse or violates higher-priority rules
- VERIFY: correctness depends on real implementation/device/server evidence

## Burden of Proof
After V18, default outcome is NO_CHANGE unless a defect is demonstrated.
